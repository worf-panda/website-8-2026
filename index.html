Problem

Existing tools focus on patient factors, but most sentinel events are attributed to provider, system, and process factors.

Increasing time constraints, distractions, population aging, patient complexity, and workforce shortages compound the problem.

THE ENGINEERING PRECEDENT

Aerospace and other high-risk industries are federally required to prove process and component safety prior to certification and commercial use. They utilize proven, standardized methods for risk analysis that ensure safety. Healthcare has no equivalent tools or requirements. That's where we come in.

Solution

Predictive clinical decision support platform that analyzes all known risk contributors and identifies the specific clinical steps most likely to precipitate adverse events.

Accounts for human factors, workflow, staffing, communication, facilities, equipment, social determinants, comorbidities, and more.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Stratify
Score risk across all workflow steps.
2. Prioritize
Surface the highest-risk steps for each patient.
3. Explain
Show why each risk is elevated.
4. Contextualize
Generate risk briefs for custom use cases.
PATENT PENDING METHOD & INNOVATION

Presaj uses proven engineering principles along with our proprietary risk topology to analyze multiple dimensions of data and identify where risks from different sources converge in the patient experience.

Explainable and auditable. AI outputs are traceable and constrained by ontologies to prevent hallucinations.

Data, not orders. We alert providers to the risks, they decide how to respond.
